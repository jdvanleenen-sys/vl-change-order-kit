What is in the kit

Change order form

Fill in the line items. It adds up cost, overhead, profit, tax and added days, then shows the new contract total. Print it, sign it.

Hidden conditions price menu

Price rot, old wiring, galvanized pipe and floor leveling once. Show the menu before demo, so a surprise is less of one.

Change order log

One line per change. Approved, pending and disputed totals sit in a side panel, with a running contract sum.

Time and material ticket

For work you cannot price up front. Hours, materials and markup on one sheet, signed the same day.

The playbook (Word)

A short guide to the sequence: stop, photograph, price, send, proceed. With phone and text scripts you can borrow.

How it works

  1. Enter your own costs and markup in the price menu. Ten minutes, once.
  2. When you find something, pause that area and take three photos.
  3. Price it from the menu and send the one-page change order.
  4. Continue when it is signed.

The prices in the menu are examples, not market rates. You enter your own costs. The kit is a business tool, not legal advice, so have your attorney review your contract language.
